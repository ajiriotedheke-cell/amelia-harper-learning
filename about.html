A little world of big adventures

Amelia Harper Kids is a family-friendly digital learning and entertainment platform bringing together playful browser games, audio bedtime stories, songs about positive everyday values, videos and printable learning resources for children and the adults who support them.

What families can explore

Children can explore age-appropriate game experiences, listen to stories, enjoy music and discover creative activities. Parents and caregivers can use the Parent Learning Centre and printable resources to extend learning beyond the screen.

Our approach

We aim to make learning inviting, creative and enjoyable. Activities should be used with adult guidance, balanced with offline play, reading, rest and movement. We continue to develop the platform and its library of original experiences.

Who operates Amelia Harper Kids?

The website is operated by Amelia Harper in Nigeria.

For parents and caregivers

Adults are the intended audience for product information, purchases, privacy information and platform enquiries. Please supervise children's use of external links and third-party services.
