FOR PARENTS AND CAREGIVERS

Privacy & safety

Information for parents and caregivers about the current website and the limits of what has been confirmed about its data practices.

Important: This is a plain-language website privacy notice, not legal advice or a certification of compliance with every applicable privacy law. It reflects the project information currently documented. The actual hosting configuration, all third-party services, retention periods and operational procedures have not been independently verified. Details not confirmed are identified as such rather than assumed.

Who operates this website?

Amelia Harper Kids is operated by Amelia Harper. The main operating country supplied for the website is the United States of America. For parent enquiries or privacy questions, contact amelia.harper@ameliaharperkids.com.

Children's use of the website

The website offers games, stories, songs and learning resources for children, including children under 13. Parents and caregivers should guide children's use, particularly when a link opens an external website or service. Product purchases and enquiries should be handled by an adult.

Gameplay events described in the project

The project documentation describes an analytics endpoint used for certain gameplay events. The fields documented for those events are:

  • Game slug and event type.
  • A broad age-band selection, rather than a date of birth.
  • A broad device category, such as mobile, tablet or desktop.
  • A country code supplied by the hosting platform, where available.
  • An event timestamp recorded by the database.

The documented game code does not intentionally send a child's name, email address, selected answers, precise location or persistent player identifier to this analytics endpoint. This statement is limited to that documented endpoint; it is not a claim that no other service or infrastructure receives technical information.

Hosting and external services

The website contains links to external services, including Gumroad for a learning resource. When a visitor follows an external link, that provider's own terms and privacy information apply. The specific technical information processed by the hosting provider, the configuration of any other third-party services, and their retention practices have not been confirmed for this notice.

Cookies, advertising and similar technologies

A complete inventory of cookies, server logs and similar technologies has not been confirmed. This notice therefore does not claim that the website or its hosting infrastructure uses none. Any future advertising, mailing-list or additional analytics integrations should be reviewed and described accurately before activation.

Retention and privacy requests

A verified data-retention period, deletion procedure and formal privacy-request workflow have not been provided for this notice. Parents and caregivers may contact amelia.harper@ameliaharperkids.com with a question or concern. Please avoid including sensitive information about a child unless it is necessary to explain the enquiry.

Changes to the website

If the website adds accounts, mailing lists, advertising, new analytics, comments, messaging or other services, the relevant data practices should be reviewed and this notice updated to reflect what is actually implemented.
