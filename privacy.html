FOR PARENTS AND CAREGIVERS

Privacy Policy

This policy explains the information associated with visits to Amelia Harper Kids, including child-directed content, advertising, gameplay events and links to external services.

Effective date: November 27, 2026

Please read: This policy is a website disclosure, not legal advice or a certification of compliance. The website operator should verify the live AdSense configuration, consent tools, hosting logs, all third-party scripts and applicable legal obligations. This page does not claim that those technical settings have been independently audited.

1. About Amelia Harper Kids

Amelia Harper Kids (ameliaharperkids.com) provides children's games, songs, audio stories and learning resources, alongside information for parents, caregivers and educators. The website is designed for children, including children under 13, and should be treated as child-directed content where applicable. Parents and caregivers are encouraged to guide children's use, particularly when an external website or purchase link is involved.

For general or privacy enquiries, use the contact details on our Contact page. The website does not publish a separate privacy-request portal.

2. Children's privacy

Children do not need to create an account to use the published games and stories. The website is not intended to ask children to submit their full name, home address, phone number or email address through game play. Purchases and direct enquiries are intended to be handled by an adult.

Parents or guardians who believe a child has sent personal information to us should contact us through the Contact page. Please do not send additional sensitive information about a child unless it is necessary to explain the concern. We will review the request and determine what action can be taken in light of the information and applicable requirements.

3. Gameplay and technical information

Certain games use a gameplay-event endpoint for basic product and performance insights. The event fields documented in the game implementation include:

  • The game identifier and event type, such as a game start or completion.
  • A broad age-band selection, not a date of birth.
  • A broad device category, such as mobile, tablet or desktop.
  • A country code supplied by the hosting platform, where available.
  • An event timestamp.

The documented gameplay event payload does not intentionally include a child's name, email address, typed answers or precise GPS location. This description is limited to the event payload; it does not mean that the website's hosting provider or other services never process technical data. Hosting and security infrastructure may process IP addresses, request details, browser information and server logs to deliver, secure and maintain the website. The exact retention periods and log settings have not been stated here.

4. Google AdSense, advertising and cookies

Amelia Harper Kids uses Google AdSense to display advertisements and support the website. Google and other technology providers involved in ad delivery may receive information from a browser when an ad is requested or displayed. Depending on the relevant service and settings, this can include the page URL, IP address, browser or device information, cookie identifiers and information about ad delivery, measurement, frequency capping and fraud prevention.

Google explains that it may use cookies and similar technologies to serve and measure ads on partner websites. These technologies can be used for purposes that include ad delivery, reporting, limiting repeated ads and, where permitted by settings and applicable rules, personalisation. Read How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and How AdSense uses cookies.

Because Amelia Harper Kids is child-directed, advertising on child-directed pages must follow Google's child-directed and publisher policies and applicable children's privacy laws. In particular, interest-based advertising and remarketing must not be used to target activity on sites or sections directed to children under 13 where COPPA applies. The publisher must correctly notify Google of child-directed content through the available designation or ad-treatment tools. This policy describes the required approach; it is not confirmation that the site's current AdSense account, Search Console designation, ad tags or every ad request have been inspected or configured correctly.

We do not ask children to click advertisements, and advertising should not be presented as a game control, reward, required step or part of a learning activity. Parents should supervise children and help them avoid following advertisements or external links.

For information about Google's publisher privacy-disclosure requirements, see Google's Privacy disclosures and Google's COPPA publisher policy. Visitors can review Google's Ad Center and available browser or device controls for advertising preferences. These controls do not replace the publisher's obligations for child-directed content.

5. External websites and services

Some pages link to services operated by other organisations, including Gumroad for printable learning products and YouTube or social platforms for family content. When you follow an external link, the other provider's privacy policy and terms apply. Those providers may collect information directly from visitors. Purchases should be completed by a parent, caregiver or other responsible adult after reviewing the product details and the provider's terms.

6. Data security and retention

We use website and hosting services to make the site available and maintain its security. No specific retention period for gameplay events, hosting logs, advertising identifiers or email enquiries is stated in this policy because a verified schedule has not been provided. Retention depends in part on the systems and service providers involved. The website operator should review and document these periods and apply an appropriate deletion process.

7. Your choices and privacy enquiries

Parents and caregivers may use the Contact page to raise a privacy question, request review of information they believe a child has submitted, or ask about this policy. Do not include passwords, payment-card details or unnecessary sensitive information in an initial enquiry. Applicable privacy rights and available choices depend on the visitor's location and the services involved.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the website's features, advertising arrangements, analytics, third-party providers or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top of the page will be revised when a material update is published.

9. Contact

For questions about this policy, please visit Contact Amelia Harper Kids. The contact page lists the current public enquiry email address.
