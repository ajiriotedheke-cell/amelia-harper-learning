FOR PARENTS AND CAREGIVERS

Privacy & safety

We want parents and caregivers to understand the website's current features and the information that has—and has not—been confirmed about its data practices.

Important scope note: This page is a plain-language website notice, not a legal opinion or confirmation of compliance with every applicable privacy law. It describes only information currently documented in the website project. Hosting, third-party services, retention, security and request-handling practices have not all been independently verified. We will not guess at those details.

Who operates this website?

Amelia Harper Kids is operated by Amelia Harper. The main operating country is Nigeria. For parent enquiries or privacy questions, contact amelia.haper@ameharperkids.com.

Children's use of the website

The website offers games, stories, songs and learning resources for children, including children under 13. Parents and caregivers should guide children's use, particularly when a link opens an external website or service. Product purchases and enquiries should be handled by an adult.

Gameplay events documented in the project

The project documentation describes an analytics endpoint used for certain gameplay events. The fields documented for those events are:

  • Game slug and event type, such as a game start or completion.
  • A broad age-band selection, rather than a date of birth.
  • A broad device category, such as mobile, tablet or desktop.
  • A country code supplied by the hosting platform, where available.
  • An event timestamp recorded by the database.

The documented game code does not intentionally send a child's name, email address, selected answers, precise location or persistent player identifier to this analytics endpoint. This statement is limited to that documented endpoint; it is not a claim that no other service or infrastructure receives technical information.

Hosting and third-party services

The website uses external services and links, including a Gumroad link for a learning resource. When visitors follow an external link, the external provider's own terms and privacy information apply. The precise technical data collected by the hosting provider, the configuration of any other third-party services, and their retention practices have not been confirmed in the information available for this notice.

Cookies, advertising and tracking

This notice does not make a blanket claim that the website or its hosting infrastructure uses no cookies, logs or similar technologies. A complete inventory of those technologies and any future advertising or analytics integrations must be checked before making such a claim. No advertising or behavioural-tracking commitments should be inferred from this page.

Retention and privacy requests

A verified retention period, deletion procedure and formal privacy-request workflow have not been provided for this notice. Parents and caregivers can contact amelia.haper@ameharperkids.com with a question or concern. Please avoid sending sensitive information about a child unless it is necessary to explain the enquiry.

Keeping this notice accurate

If the website adds new accounts, mailing lists, advertising, analytics, comments, messaging or other services, the relevant data practices should be reviewed and this notice updated before those features are introduced.
