## Suggested four-week publishing rhythm

- Week 1: Publish two alphabet activity pins and one matching game pin.
- Week 2: Publish bedtime routine and story pins; share the story video only if the final video is live.
- Week 3: Publish sharing/kindness and Pet Planet pins.
- Week 4: Publish screen-time and dinosaur activity pins, then review click-throughs and saves.

## Before publishing

- Confirm each destination URL loads on mobile.
- Use original or properly licensed images, music and video.
- Do not claim a resource is free unless it is actually free.
- Do not publish song-lyrics pins or song videos until the corresponding track and authorised lyrics are released.
- For child-directed YouTube videos, select the appropriate Made for Kids audience setting.
- Do not add behavioural advertising, tracking pixels or mailing-list forms to the website as part of this promotion without a separate privacy and child-directed compliance review.
