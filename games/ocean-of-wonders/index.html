Underwater exploration in Ocean of Wonders

Ocean of Wonders is a 3D underwater game. Players guide Bubbles through an ocean environment and collect golden coins. The game offers a playful opportunity to practise directional movement, visual attention and exploring a digital environment.

Make the ocean adventure a learning conversation

Ask your child to name sea creatures they recognise, describe the colours of the reef or imagine what might live near coral. Follow the game with a picture book or drawing activity about ocean habitats.
