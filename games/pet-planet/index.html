A little care makes a big difference. Try to keep all four needs healthy. This is a pretend pet-care game — no real animal is involved.

A virtual pet game about care and kindness

Pet Planet features Mochi, a virtual pet children can feed, give water, brush and play with. The activities create opportunities for an adult and child to talk about routines, caring for living things and noticing what a character might need.

Talk about responsibility together

Ask what Mochi needs and why. Connect the pretend-care activity to real examples of kindness, such as helping put toys away or watering a plant with adult supervision. A virtual pet is a play experience and does not replace caring for a real animal.
