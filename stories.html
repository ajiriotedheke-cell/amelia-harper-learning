About this story

Tumi and the Little Star is presented as a gentle listening adventure beneath a twinkling sky. This page currently provides a short description rather than a written transcript.

Talk about the story together

  • What do you remember most about Tumi's adventure?
  • What did you imagine when you heard about the little star?
  • Which part felt calm, surprising or interesting?
  • If you could add a new scene, what would happen next?
